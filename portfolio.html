May–Aug 2025

Building and measuring an MIS capacitor

NE 340L Microfabrication lab · University of Waterloo

The problem

Make a simple chip device, a metal–insulator–semiconductor capacitor, from a bare silicon wafer, then check whether the films came out the way we intended.

What I did

In a three-person lab group, I did the hands-on fabrication steps: depositing a silicon-nitride film by PECVD, two rounds of photolithography (spin, bake, expose, develop), reactive ion etching of the nitride, and wet-etching the aluminum to form the electrodes. The instructor and TA handled the initial wafer clean and the aluminum sputtering. I wrote my own individual report with the analysis.

What I learned Every measurement tool has limits. Measuring the same film three ways taught me to ask how a number was measured before trusting it.

Read more

Process steps

  1. Wafer clean (instructor)
  2. PECVD SiNx
  3. Photolithography, Mask 1
  4. RIE of SiNx
  5. Al sputtering (TA/instructor)
  6. Photolithography, Mask 2 (aligned)
  7. Al wet etch
  8. Photoresist strip
  9. Thickness and electrical measurements
